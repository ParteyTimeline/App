You can now export a playlist (including its offline cache) and import
it again elsewhere — handy for moving playlists between devices or
sharing them with another host. A new "Manage playlists" screen also
lets you rename a playlist, clear just its cache, or delete it.

Fixed: starting to host without Wi-Fi no longer hides the "Continue to
game" button, and switching to "Find game nearby" now actually stops
hosting instead of running both at once.

Hosts can now stop accepting new players without shutting down the
whole game — already-connected guests get disconnected too, and their
app falls back to the start screen instead of sitting on a dead page.
