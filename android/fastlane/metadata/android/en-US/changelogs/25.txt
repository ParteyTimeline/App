Bug and security fixes found during a closer review of the previous
release: a reconnect counter that could get stuck and retry forever, a
"permission needed" message that could get cleared too eagerly, and
denying the notification permission no longer blocks hosting or joining.
Also hardens the embedded server (used for Nearby/LAN play) against
several issues, including a passwordless-join loophole on non-Android
deployments and a few ways malformed input could crash it. No new
features, and no changes to how a game looks or plays.
