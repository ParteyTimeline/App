Fixes from a deeper follow-up review of the previous release: a reconnect
give-up notification that could fire while the app kept quietly retrying,
and a stale callback from an abandoned connection attempt that could
briefly interfere with a newer one. Also closes an IPv6 gap in the
embedded server's cover-image safety check and an identity mix-up between
a Nearby/LAN guest and a newly registered account sharing the same name.
No new features, and no changes to how a game looks or plays.
