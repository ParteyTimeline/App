More fixes from continued review of the Nearby reconnect logic and the
embedded server's cover-image safety check: a rare case where an
abandoned connection attempt could still affect a newer one for longer
than it should, and a narrow timing window where closing one connection
could disconnect a different, already-replaced connection. Also closes a
remaining loophole in the cover-image safety check for a differently
spelled form of "localhost". No new features, and no changes to how a
game looks or plays.
